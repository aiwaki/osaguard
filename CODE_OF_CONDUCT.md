# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want OsaGuard to be a welcoming, constructive project for people of every
background and experience level. Participation must remain free from harassment,
intimidation, and discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while still discussing technical evidence
  directly.
- Give actionable feedback about work, not personal attacks about contributors.
- Respect privacy, especially around security reports and authentication data.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and disclosure.
- Help make documentation and discussion understandable to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, stalking, sexualized attention, or discriminatory remarks;
- insults, trolling, sustained disruption, or publishing someone's private data;
- pressuring a reporter to disclose a vulnerability publicly;
- knowingly posting credentials, exploit details under embargo, or harmful test
  material;
- retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code. Decisions
will consider context, severity, impact, and whether harmful behavior continues.

Report conduct issues privately to the repository owner through GitHub. If no
private contact option is available, open an issue titled `Conduct contact
requested` without personal or incident details. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical. For platform-level abuse or immediate safety
concerns, use GitHub's own abuse-reporting tools.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project.
